Corporate Card, Travel Cash Advance & Expense Claim Policy

WMI-SOP-FIN-AP-04Rev 2 · Effective 01/07/2025 · Covers: Corporate Card, Travel Cash Advances, Concur Expense Reimbursement

1. Corporate Card

  • Eligibility: permanent WMI employees with high travel/subsistence/entertainment spend. Not eligible: contractors, temporary/fixed-term, students.
  • Applying: request form from Treasury → complete with supporting docs → approved locally by BU Manager/Director + Head of Finance. (Full process: see WMI-SOP-FIN-TR-04.)
  • Limit adjustment: cardholder request w/ justification → BU Manager/Director approval → Finance Team reviews (credit facility availability, usage history) → full/partial approval or rejection → Administrator implements.

Restrictions

  • No IT equipment/inventory/supplies/cleaning services (use PO via Coupa) unless small value agreed with Finance & Accounting.
  • Never share/loan the card; never leave card/account number exposed.
  • Only permanent WMI employees; never used to withdraw cash; never split transactions to bypass limits.
  • Unintentional personal charge: notify Manager + Head of Dept + AP&Treasury team immediately → enter in Concur as "Personal Expense" → reimburse WMI via bank transfer.
  • Fraud/misuse → card revocation + possible termination.

Lifecycle

  • Lost/stolen: notify bank + Corporate Card Administrator (AP&Treasury) immediately.
  • Usage review every 2 years — inactive accounts auto-closed.
  • Resignation: settle all transactions, return the physical card to Finance before leaving.

Transactions, receipts & timescales

  • Receipts required for ALL expenses in Concur (handwritten/no-stamp receipts not acceptable → use Missing Receipt Affidavit).
  • Receipt must show: amount, date, place, description, business purpose, attendee identities (meals/entertainment), itemized meal receipt.
  • Submit monthly; due within 30 days after expense report period end. Manager approval within 20 calendar days of statement date.

Escalation — Corporate Card / Expense report overdue

30–60 daysBusiness Unit Manager/Head
60–90 daysBU Manager/Head & Head of Finance
>90 daysManaging Director
>120 daysCard suspended (reinstatement needs Regional/Division Finance Director approval) + HR disciplinary action (negligence)

2. Travel Cash Advance

  • Proposed prior to travel via Concur. Format: TAR Number – Report Title (e.g. "TAR 12345 – Travel to Freeport Meeting Customer"). Requires: employee name, travel purpose/locations/duration, justification for advance, amount + currency.
  • Employee limited to 1 active cash advance at a time (exceptions: stranded in remote area, or extended travel directly to next site — needs BU Manager/Director + Head of Finance approval).
  • Settlement: within 30 days after travel completion; same escalation ladder as above. If shortfall vs. advance → employee reimbursed extra; if surplus → employee returns difference. Must also return advance if trip cancelled/postponed or employee resigns with outstanding advance.

3. Expense Claim

  • Only WMI employees use Concur for reimbursement (non-WMI/outsourced must invoice via third-party vendor).
  • Settlement window: 90 days after report date. >90–180 days: BU Manager/Head approval; >180 days: BU Manager/Head + Managing Director.
  • Report formats: Travel Expense Report (TAR Number – Title), Department Operational Report, Specific Event Report (same formats as AP-03).

Claimable

Train/bus/taxi + tips; business tolls & parking; laundry (trips >2 days); hotel/airfare tips ≤15% (only if company can't issue ticket directly); meals + tip ≤15%; personal-phone business calls; FX fees on business spend abroad; baggage wrapping; courier for business docs; job-relevant subscriptions; company-vehicle fuel/oil (official receipt, prevailing unsubsidized rate); personal-car travel per Company Vehicle Policy; small ops expenditures approved by Finance; standard-class lounge access if layover ≥6 hours.

Non-claimable (some *reimbursable only with Special Approval)

Babysitter, airline club dues, barber/hairstylist, traffic/parking fines, tips >15%/double-tipping, adult/personal entertainment (magazines, tours, cinema, spa, massage), in-house movies, cigarettes, alcohol (except client entertainment), luggage/briefcases*, personal annual card fees, toiletries/beauty*, outfits*, USB/phone accessories*, over-baggage*, stationery*, tools*, insurance-covered medicine*, safety attributes*, car rental*, Coupa-manageable purchases*.

5. Other rules

  • Hotel/flight: normally booked by GA Dept; employee may self-book via Concur only for urgent/weekend cases with GA approval, within WMI Travel Policy rate (excess needs line manager + GA approval, else employee/company absorbs per limit).
  • Entertainment (external): gifts <£50 acknowledged by Dept Head + pre-approved GM Ops & Head of Finance; >£50 needs Global Delegated Authority Matrix approval. Must file WMI-FORM-FIN-AP-02 (Nominative List of Entertainment Expense) for tax. See Resources.
  • Entertainment (internal/staff): allowed grade 2B+; below that needs special approval from Dept Head.
  • Company car: Operational (pool, PIC min Band 2C) vs Entitled (Manager 2A+). Fuel/standard maintenance claimable monthly; excessive maintenance (salon/premium wash) not claimable unless justified. Operational-vehicle users must attach WMI-FORM-FIN-AP-01 Monthly Vehicle Usage Report (not required for Entitled 2A+ holders).
  • Personal car (business use): needs manager approval + insurance info (not required for home↔office). Must submit WMI-FORM-FIN-AP-03 Personal Car Mileage Rate Report (car type/year, license no., fuel ratio, employee ID, trip purpose, fuel-ratio screenshot, STNK photo, Google Maps route screenshot, latest fuel invoice, insurance if applicable). Fuel claimable at reasonable cost; maintenance not claimable; company doesn't cover insurance/repair/risk.
  • Tolls/parking: e-toll/flash-card transaction history accepted as proof; else original receipt image in Concur.
  • Missing receipt: file Missing Receipt Declaration in Concur (receipt lost, no official stamp, or merchant genuinely can't issue one — avoid where possible). Quarterly review/escalation on missing-receipt frequency per employee.
  • Special Approval: non-reimbursable-by-default expense needs written line-manager approval (email/chat) with reason, attached in Concur — else not reimbursable.

6. FX rate for overseas expenses (no corporate card used)

  1. Personal credit card → use rate on official bank statement (attach statement).
  2. Cash FX → use money-changer receipt rate (attach receipt); resulting FX gain/loss claimable if documented.
  3. No card/receipt → use Bank Indonesia kurs jual (selling rate) as of end of month prior to trip start; any FX difference borne by employee.
