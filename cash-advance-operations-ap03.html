Cash Advance Operations Policy

WMI-SOP-FIN-AP-03Rev 1 · Effective 01/07/2025 · Scope: all WMI employees proposing operational cash advance

Purpose & when it applies

Covers small, incidental operational expenditures that cannot practically go through bank transfer (urgent/low-value purchases, unregistered/local vendors, employees without a P-Card/Corporate Card, locations with limited payment channels), processed via Concur.

Governance workflow

  1. Pre-approval (before first Concur submission): employee submits purpose, transaction nature, amounts, business justification.
  2. Finance review: evaluates nature of transaction and alternatives (listed vendors, direct Finance payment).
  3. Approval chain: Cost Centre Owner (Manager) → Head of Finance → Managing Director (only if required by policy).

Recurring cash advance

  • Same amount can be re-requested monthly without repeating pre-approval, provided the prior advance is fully reported/reconciled and scope/amount is unchanged.
  • Monthly report deadline: by end of first week of the following month.

Escalation — recurring report overdue

>30 daysRequestor restricted from new cash advance; warning to line manager
30–60 daysEscalate to Business Unit Manager/Head
60–90 daysEscalate to BU Manager/Head & Head of Finance
>90 daysEscalate to Managing Director

If already settled but reimbursement to company delayed >30 days after submission: Finance instructs HR to recover via payroll (employee copied on the email).

Limits

  • Standard recurring limit: IDR 10,000,000 max.
  • Above IDR 10M: requires justification + breakdown, approved per company approval matrix (e.g. Dept Head + Head of Finance).
  • Semi-annual review of all advances ≥ IDR 10M; increases require historical-usage justification + Head of Finance approval.

Non-recurring / exceptional cash advance

  • One-time disbursement for non-routine business-critical needs (events, workshops, urgent business transactions) where PO/corporate card is not feasible.
  • Must be discussed with Purchasing & Finance before submission.
  • Requires pre-approval by Managing Director.
  • Meals for events: approvable up to Department Head, unless amount > IDR 25,000,000 (then MD pre-approval required).

Restrictions / exclusions from cash advance

  • Payments to government bodies that cannot be vendor-registered — pay directly by bank transfer via Payment Request Application, no time limit.
  • Medical expenses — go through Concur with line manager/BU head pre-approval (unless invoice truly unobtainable at that location).
  • Any travel expense — must use WMI-SOP-FIN-AP-04 instead.
  • Uncovered grey-area transactions require Finance Department consultation; no line-manager/Finance acknowledgement = not accepted.
