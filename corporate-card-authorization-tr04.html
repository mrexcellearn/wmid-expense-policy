Corporate Card Authorization

WMI-SOP-FIN-TR-04Rev 1 · Effective Dec 2025 · Governs Treasury-side issuance process, references AP-04

Application process

  1. Employee requests application form from Treasury; approval needed from BU Manager/Director + Head of Finance & Accounting.
  2. Applicant completes form + supporting docs (name, title, DOB, ID number, nationality, mother's maiden name, home/office address, office email, role, phone, staff ID, preferred card name ≤21 chars, scanned ID/passport).
  3. Treasury completes HSBC Corporate Card Form → submits to bank.
  4. Processing time: 2 weeks – 1 month depending on bank review; delays >1 month communicated to employee.

Limit & adjustment

Initial limit set by Finance & Accounting based on forecast monthly spend. Adjustment workflow mirrors WMI-SOP-FIN-AP-04: cardholder request (with BU Manager/Director approval) → Finance Team review (credit facility availability, usage history) → full/partial/rejected decision → Administrator implements.

Lost/stolen, review, resignation

  • Lost/stolen: call HSBC Phone Banking 1500700 (24h) + notify Treasury immediately for closure.
  • Usage review every 2 years; inactive accounts auto-closed.
  • Resignation: settle all outstanding transactions before card closure; unsettled amounts deducted from final salary via HR.

Expense settlement & restrictions

Same 30-day submission window, escalation ladder (30-60-90-120 days), and restriction list as AP-04 Section 1 (no IT/inventory/cleaning purchases outside PO, no lending/sharing card, permanent employees only, no cash withdrawal, unintentional personal-charge process identical). See full AP-04 policy.

Attachments

  • Corporate Card Proposal Form (filled by requestor)
  • HSBC Corporate Card Setup Form (filled by Treasury — company info, card administrators, cardholder KTP/passport, staff ID, card limit, signatures)
